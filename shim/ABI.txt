Soloist Pulse client ABI
========================

Soloist 1.3.7 (CDN x86_64 1.3.7.474; also 1.3.7.472) does not DT_NEEDED
libpulse. It dlopens libpulse.so.0 and looks up these names. That list is
the spec. libpulse-simple and libpulse-mainloop-glib are not used.
